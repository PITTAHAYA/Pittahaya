What everyone asks.

Why don't you publish prices?

Because every project is different: the number of pages, the type of design and the level of customization all change the investment. In the strategy call we define the scope and you receive a clear, no-strings-attached proposal.

Do I need copy and images ready?

It's not required. If you don't have copy, we'll help you build the message: value proposition, benefits and calls to action. Images can come from licensed stock libraries or be combined with your own.

Does it include domain and hosting?

It depends on the plan. In most cases we help you set up your domain and hosting, or connect the site to what you already have. We clarify this in the strategy call so everything is organized from day one.

Can I add AI later?

Yes. Automation plans work on their own or added to any web plan, so you can start with your website and add the assistant when you need it. The Premium plan already includes an AI chatbot.

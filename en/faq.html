Questions

What would youlike to know?

01How much does a premium website cost?

Pricing depends on scope: number of pages, design type, and level of customization. The fastest way to know is to fill out the strategy call form — you'll receive a clear, no-strings-attached proposal.

02How long until my site is ready?

It depends on scope and review pace. A high-impact landing can be ready in a few days; a full multi-page site can take a couple of weeks. We define real timelines from the start so there are no surprises.

03Do I need to have copy and images ready?

It's not required. If you don't have copy, we'll help you build the message: value proposition, benefits, and calls to action. Images can come from licensed stock libraries or be combined with your own.

04Will I be able to edit my site afterwards?

Yes. We can deliver clear instructions to edit basic content, or set up an ongoing support plan for periodic changes. We adapt it to your technical comfort and needs.

05Does it include domain and hosting?

It depends on the plan you choose. In most cases we help you set up your domain and hosting, or connect the site to what you already have. We clarify this in the initial strategy call so everything is organized from day one.

06What makes Pittahaya different?

The design is custom from scratch — not a template filled with your data. Every site is built around your offer, your ideal client, and the result you need to achieve. The goal isn't "to have a website," it's to have a sales tool that works 24/7.

07Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. We work with entrepreneurs and companies across Latin America, Canada, and the US. The process is 100% remote: diagnosis, design, revisions, and delivery happen digitally, regardless of your location.

08How many revisions does the process include?

It depends on the plan: 1 round on Starter, 2 on Business and 3 on Premium. Each round covers copy, structure and visual details, so you know from day one exactly what your project includes.

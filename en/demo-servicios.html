Tree to bar

Forty-seven days between the pod and you.

Six steps, none of them rushed. These are the real dates of Latitud Cero lot LC-2614, from the Cedeño farm to our tempering table.

HarvestDay 0 · Ripe pods, opened on the farm the same day.
FermentationDays 1–6 · In laurel-wood boxes under banana leaves. This is where the raspberry is born.
DryingDays 7–15 · In the sun, turned by hand every two hours.
RoastDay 32 · 118 °C for 32 minutes: just enough to keep the jasmine.
ConchingDays 40–43 · 72 hours of refining, until not a single grain is left.
TemperingDay 47 · Moulded, numbered, and sealed by hand in Quito.
